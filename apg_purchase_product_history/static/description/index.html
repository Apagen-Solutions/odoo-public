Purchase History of Products

Purpose: This module allows you to view the complete purchase history of products directly from the product form in Odoo. You can configure purchase history settings, set record limits, and filter by purchase status such as All, RFQ, or Purchase Order.

Key Features

  • Complete Purchase History View all past purchases of a product, including vendor, date, quantity, and price.
  • Configurable Settings Set history record limits and choose the purchase status to display.
  • Status Filters Filter purchase history by All, RFQ, or Purchase Order.
  • Quick Access Access purchase history directly from the product view without extra navigation.

How It Works:

  • Go to Settings > Purchase > Product Purchase History.
  • Set the record limit (0 for unlimited).
  • Select the status filter: All, RFQ, or Purchase Order.
  • Open any product form to view the purchase history as per your settings.

Benefits:

Quick insight into past purchases
Better supplier negotiation with historical data
Time-saving direct access from product form
