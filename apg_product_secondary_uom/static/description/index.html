Product Secondary Unit of Measure

Use Secondary UOM in Sales and Purchase Orders

This module allows users to define and use a Secondary Unit of Measure (UOM) for products. Once installed, users can enable secondary UOM at the product level, which will then be available for use in Sale Order Lines and Purchase Order Lines.

Key Features:

  • Adds a new option to enable Secondary UOM on the product form
  • Allows defining a specific secondary UOM for each product
  • Displays the secondary UOM in Sales Order Lines and Purchase Order Lines
  • Improves flexibility for businesses dealing with multiple measurement units

Sales/Orders-->Orders -> New

Purchase/Orders -> Purchase Orders -> New

Inventory/Products -> Products--> New

Usage:

Go to the Products menu and open any product form. Enable the Secondary UOM checkbox and choose the desired UOM. Once set, this UOM will be available for selection and display in sales and purchase transactions.

Technical Details:

  • Compatible with Odoo 18
  • Integrates seamlessly with Sales and Purchase modules
  • Does not affect primary UOM configuration
